# Cortex Engine Retained Baseline

**Snapshot date:** 2026-07-26  
**Scope:** The Cortex design and results that remain after CP20  
**Implementation status:** This is the integrated baseline description. The corresponding integrated production engine has not yet been built.

This document is the single current description of Cortex. Earlier checkpoint reports, ADR collections, rule packages, and research ledgers remain useful as source evidence and regression fixtures, but they are not needed to understand what Cortex is.

The present project position is:

```text
CP20 causal and sparse resident/evictable-world runtime
+ complete lifted object and Orthad
+ v22 compact exact execution
+ retained emergence observatories
- production integration of the complete Orthad into the CP20 kernel
```

## 1. What Cortex is

Cortex is a specialized Phase Calculus engine. It is not a generic simulation shell with a replaceable physics kernel.

The connectome is the retained world representation. It contains stable local cells, nodes, relations or edges, travelers, carried tokens, local world state, and the causal dependencies that join them.

The engine has no global simulation tick, no scheduler that assigns `B`, `Q`, or `L`, and no graph-wide sweep. A local region changes only when it participates in a lawful cause. Regions outside that cause remain exactly unchanged.

The lifted object supplies the native retained geometry. Cortex does not begin by installing known physical structures into the connectome. It executes the lifted recurrence, retains its consequences, and permits read-only observatories to recognize structure generated by that recurrence.

Learning is retained causal world-state evolution. Cortex has no separate training phase, optimizer-owned weights, or learned-state archive outside the world that continues.

CF000 and CF00 remain Phase Calculus. $B/Q/L$ is the operational grammar through which the retained construction advances; it is not a replacement taxonomy. $Q$ opens the next phase position. $L$ latches the completed active geometry into retained history and extends the construction into a new domain. Cortex introduces no post-CF19 classification scheme.

## 2. Exact notation and mathematical objects

Latin $X$-based forms are scratch notation only. They do not name additional states.

The lifted state carried by primitive custody is:

$$
\Xi_t=\left(
A_t,\,
(u_t,v_t),\,
\theta_t,\,
k_t,\,
j_t,\,
W_t
\right).
$$

The carried pair, phase value, and scalar obey:

$$
q_t=(u_t,v_t),
\qquad
1\le u_t\le v_t,
\qquad
\theta_t\in\{1,i,-1,-i\},
\qquad
a_t=\frac{\theta_t}{u_tv_t}.
$$

The retained coordinate $\theta_t$ is the unit phase value, not an angle. If
an angular parameter $\vartheta_t$ is useful for exposition, then:

$$
\theta_t=e^{i\vartheta_t}.
$$

This introduces no additional state coordinate. It only keeps the two
notations type-correct.

The coordinates mean:

- $A_t$ is the current domain.
- $(u_t,v_t)$ is the carried refinement pair.
- $\theta_t$ is the carried phase.
- $k_t$ is the domain-local phase position.
- $j_t$ is the global phase-position index.
- $W_t$ is the exact ordered primitive history.

The occurrence of $W_t$ in the formal state is a mathematical statement, not
a storage instruction. It says that every finite prefix has one exact ordered
history and that an exact word view can be regenerated. Production continuation
does not require a flat word buffer, replay of prior letters, or a second archive
of history beside the geometry. The retained geometry and custody coordinates
already contain the consequences of that history.

The complete lifted object is:

$$
\widehat{\Xi}_t=\left(
\Xi_t,\,
P_t,\,
\Omega_t^+,\,
\Omega_t^-,\,
T_t^{+\to-},\,
T_t^{-\to+}
\right).
$$

The Orthad is written with its asymmetric lower-left and upper-right enclosure. The functional form is an equivalent display of the same Orthad:

$$
\boxed{
\llcorner\widehat{\Xi}_t\urcorner
=\mathcal O(\widehat{\Xi}_t)
=\begin{pmatrix}
\Omega_t^+ & T_t^{-\to+}\\
T_t^{+\to-} & \Omega_t^-
\end{pmatrix}
}.
$$

These three symbols have different meanings:

- $\Xi_t$ is the lifted state carried by primitive custody. It selects the next primitive from its own current coordinates.
- $\widehat{\Xi}_t$ is the complete lifted object: custody, the primary relation, both opposed charts, and both directed transfers.
- $\llcorner\widehat{\Xi}_t\urcorner=\mathcal O(\widehat{\Xi}_t)$ is the Orthad: the active dual-chart geometry carried by the complete lifted object.

The Orthad is not the lifted object itself, an external wrapper, a controller, or a primitive selector. It mutates with the lifted object on every primitive transition.

The lifted object is not merely the pair, the word, a matrix, or a scalar. It is the entire recursively retained state: autonomous primitive custody together with the exact layered primary relation, its two opposed chart presentations, and their two directed transfers.

The components are:

- $P_t$: the common primary relation over all retained points.
- $\Omega_t^+$: the positive chart restriction of $P_t$.
- $\Omega_t^-$: the opposed negative chart restriction of $P_t$.
- $T_t^{+\to-}$: the directed positive-to-negative transfer block.
- $T_t^{-\to+}$: the directed negative-to-positive transfer block.

The construction is pairing-first. The charts and transfers are not independently invented objects later attached to the relation. With explicit chart maps $\iota_+$ and $\iota_-$, their dependency is:

$$
\Omega_t^+=\iota_+^*P_t\iota_+,
\qquad
\Omega_t^-=\iota_-^*P_t\iota_-,
$$

$$
T_t^{+\to-}=\iota_-^*P_t\iota_+,
\qquad
T_t^{-\to+}=\iota_+^*P_t\iota_-.
$$

This complete object replaces CP20's reduced active-axis stand-in. The primary relation, opposed charts, and directed transfers are no longer missing mathematics.

## 3. Primitive custody and exact Orthad mutation

### Primitive custody and exact Orthad mutation

There is no external primitive selector, scheduler, controller, policy, or host law.

At each lawful local transition, the current lifted state $\Xi_t$ itself forces exactly one primitive:

$$
U_t\in{B,Q,L}.
$$

The symbol $U_t$ names the primitive forced by the current state and acting during that transition. It is not a separate mechanism that examines $\Xi_t$, and it is not supplied by the admitted cause.

The recurrence applies its exact priority internally:

$$
B>Q>L.
$$

Thus $B$ occurs whenever its continuation condition is satisfied. If $B$ is blocked and another phase position remains, $Q$ occurs. If $B$ is blocked at the final phase position, $L$ occurs.

After every primitive transition, the complete successor $\widehat{\Xi}*{t+1}$ is retained before its new state forces the following primitive $U*{t+1}$. There is no alternation rule, imposed $B^*Q^*L$ block, or macro word that schedules the recurrence.


For Domain $A$, the number of phase positions and the first global phase-position index are:

$$
N(A)=6\cdot2^A,
\qquad
J_{\mathrm{start}}(A)=1+6(2^A-1).
$$

The position invariant is:

$$
0\le k<N(A),
\qquad
j=J_{\mathrm{start}}(A)+k.
$$

### Capacity

$$
\Delta(j)=
\begin{cases}
2,&j=1,\\
4,&j=2,\\
2^{2j},&j\ge 3.
\end{cases}
$$

Domain 0 therefore exposes:

$$
2,\ 4,\ 64,\ 256,\ 1024,\ 4096.
$$

### $B$: balanced refinement

$$
B(u,v)=(v,u+v).
$$

At an ordinary phase position $k<N(A)-1$, let:

$$
(u',v')=B(u,v).
$$

$B$ is admitted when:

$$
u'v'\le \Delta(j).
$$

At the final phase position $k=N(A)-1$, the boundary test instead uses the current pair:

$$
uv<\Delta(j).
$$

This permits the final refinement to cross the current capacity floor before $L$.

If the active layer begins:

$$
D_A=(0,x,p_2,\ldots),
$$

the same $B$ inserts:

$$
x_{\mathrm{new}}=\frac{u}{u+v}x
$$

at the active front. Older determinations remain retained.

The $B$ transition therefore:

$$
(u,v)\mapsto(v,u+v),
\qquad
W\mapsto WB,
$$

while $A,\theta,k,j$ and every completed layer remain unchanged.

### $Q$: quarter turn and capacity advance

$Q$ occurs only when $B$ is blocked and $k<N(A)-1$. It leaves $(u,v)$ unchanged and performs:

$$
\theta\mapsto i\theta,
\qquad
k\mapsto k+1,
\qquad
j\mapsto j+1,
\qquad
W\mapsto WQ.
$$

Equivalently, if the phase is written with the optional angular parameter,
$\vartheta\mapsto\vartheta+\pi/2$. The carried phase value moves to the next
member of $1,i,-1,-i$. The new $j$ exposes the next capacity.

The same $Q$ multiplies every point in the active layer by $i$:

$$
(p_0,p_1,\ldots,p_{n-1})
\mapsto
(ip_0,ip_1,\ldots,ip_{n-1}).
$$

Completed layers remain unchanged.

The retained origin remains $0$, since $i\cdot0=0$.

### $L$: latch and extend

$L$ occurs only when $B$ is blocked and $k=N(A)-1$, so there is no unused $Q$ position.

Its exact custody update is:

$$
\left(A,(u,v),\theta,k,j,W\right)
\mapsto
\left(
A+1,\,
(u,v),\,
\theta,\,
0,\,
J_{\mathrm{start}}(A+1),\,
WL
\right).
$$

During that same transition:

- the pair and phase keep exactly the values they already had;
- the prior logical word remains the unchanged prefix of $WL$;
- the complete active layer becomes the newest immutable completed layer;
- every older completed layer remains unchanged;
- one new active layer opens at $(0,1)$.

Here, “keeps,” “remains,” and “carries” describe equality and persistence of
mathematical state. They do not require copying the pair, rewriting completed
layers, serializing the prior word, replaying prior primitives, or duplicating
history in memory. In a compact implementation, $L$ updates the domain boundary
descriptor, closes the active structural descriptor, and installs the new
two-point active seed. The completed geometry itself is the retained prior
history. The word extension $W\mapsto WL$ remains exactly reproducible without
being stored as an ever-growing production array.

This is extension, not reset. Every completed layer remains present.

### One coupled transition

For the self-selected $U_t\in\{B,Q,L\}$:

$$
\widehat{\Xi}_t
\xrightarrow{\ U_t\ }
\widehat{\Xi}_{t+1},
\qquad
\llcorner\widehat{\Xi}_t\urcorner
\xrightarrow{\ U_t\ }
\llcorner\widehat{\Xi}_{t+1}\urcorner.
$$

The dependency order inside this one atomic transition is:

1. $\Xi_t$ alone selects $U_t$.
2. One coupled state function computes the complete successor:

   $$
   \widehat{\Xi}_{t+1}=F_{U_t}(\widehat{\Xi}_t).
   $$

   “The same primitive mutates every component” means that no component receives
   a separately chosen operation. It does not mean that a compact engine must
   visit and rewrite every implied point or relation entry.
3. The coupled result is exact for the selected primitive:

   | Primitive | Coupled custody and Orthad result |
   |---|---|
   | $B$ | Refine $(u,v)$, insert one new active determination, extend both chart readings and their reversal maps, and add exactly the primary-relation placements involving the new point. Completed layers remain unchanged. |
   | $Q$ | Advance phase and position, multiply every active-layer determination by $i$, and carry that same coordinate change through both chart readings and every primary-relation placement incident to the active layer. Relation incidence and cardinality do not change. Completed layers remain unchanged. |
   | $L$ | Advance the domain, latch the complete active layer, open $(0,1)$, extend both chart presentations and both reversal maps, and add the new layer’s within relation plus both directed cross-layer products. All prior relation placements remain unchanged. |

   These are structural updates. A production representation may update only
   the sufficient descriptors from which the exact entries are reconstructed.
4. Both directed chart transfers are current in that same successor. Transfer
   reconciliation is not another primitive and cannot influence selection.
5. The complete $\widehat{\Xi}_{t+1}$ is retained.
6. Only then may $\Xi_{t+1}$ select the next primitive.

There is no intermediate retained state in which the primary relation is current while either chart or transfer is stale.

## 4. Exact retained geometry

The Orthad begins with one active ordered layer:

$$
D_0=(0,1).
$$

The first point is the retained origin. The first nonzero point is the active local determination. Later $B$ operations insert new determinations without overwriting older ones.

For a layer:

$$
D=(p_0,p_1,\ldots,p_{n-1}),
$$

the forward chart is:

$$
C^+(D)=(p_0,p_1,\ldots,p_{n-1}),
$$

and the reverse chart is:

$$
C^-(D)=(p_{n-1},p_{n-2},\ldots,p_0).
$$

The transfers are exact inverses:

$$
T^{+\to-}(p_r)=p_{n-1-r},
\qquad
T^{-\to+}(p_{n-1-r})=p_r.
$$

Their domains and codomains are the two chart copies:

$$
T^{+\to-}:C^+(D)\to C^-(D),
\qquad
T^{-\to+}:C^-(D)\to C^+(D).
$$

Therefore:

$$
T^{-\to+}\circ T^{+\to-}=\operatorname{id}_{C^+(D)},
\qquad
T^{+\to-}\circ T^{-\to+}=\operatorname{id}_{C^-(D)}.
$$

If the two chart copies are identified with their common ordered layer, both
directions use the reversal involution:

$$
R_D(r)=n-1-r,
\qquad
R_D(R_D(r))=r.
$$

Thus “inverse” does not mean that the negative chart runs a different primitive
or reverses causal progression. One selected $B$, $Q$, or $L$ changes the
underlying retained object once; the two transfer blocks preserve the exact,
reversible correspondence between its counter-oriented chart presentations.
For odd $n$, the middle position is the fixed point of the involution.

The two charts are counter-oriented and overset. Neither is globally privileged. Their overlap is handled by the two directed transfers and does not create a third chart. 

Within one layer:

$$
P(D)=\{(p_r,p_s):r<s\}.
$$

For distinct layers:

$$
P_{ab}=D_a\times D_b,\qquad a\ne b.
$$

The cross-layer blocks are directed and exhaustive. $P_{ab}$ and $P_{ba}$ are both retained; no ungenerated selector removes placements from either Cartesian product.

For layer sizes $n_0,\ldots,n_r$, exact relation cardinalities are:

$$
S=\sum_a n_a,\qquad Q_2=\sum_a n_a^2,
$$

$$
\text{within}=\frac{Q_2-S}{2},
$$

$$
\text{directed cross}=S^2-Q_2,
$$

$$
\text{total relation}=\text{within}+\text{directed cross}.
$$

These formulas count the complete typed relation. They do not replace it. Any requested point, chart image, transfer image, or relation placement is reconstructed exactly from the retained structural state and deterministic recurrence.

## 5. Structural retention without eager expansion

Logical retention and physical materialization are different.

Cortex retains the complete lifted object structurally. It does not store every implied point and relation entry as a separate hot-path record.

Production continuation uses compact exact descriptors that are sufficient to:

- select the next primitive;
- update custody and Orthad structure;
- preserve completed-layer immutability;
- reconstruct exact finite views;
- compute exact cardinalities;
- detect capacity demand before mutation;
- snapshot, restore, and continue exactly.

$W_t$ remains the exact mathematical history. Production does not retain it as an ever-growing byte array and does not replay it to recover current state. A primitive receipt is transient. Exact finite word views remain reproducible for testing and audit.

Production transitions do not clone the complete hierarchy, serialize the full state, hash the full state, or validate every implied relation on every primitive.

Cortex does not compute or retain a runtime hash, digest, fingerprint, or
checksum of its state. The canonical retained bytes are the state; stable entity identity,
generation, and exact version identify which state a cause addresses. A
byte-for-byte comparison may compare those bytes directly when comparison is
actually required.

Research archives may use file hashes to verify that an external artifact was
copied unchanged. Those hashes remain outside Cortex source state, snapshots,
primitive selection, causal admission, and continuation. Fully materialized
views and exhaustive validators are likewise external research surfaces.

Exact growth is explicit. When the current fixed representation is insufficient, the transition reports the required capacity without mutation. Provisioning and retry use the same cause and cannot duplicate the result.

### Compact exact boundary selector

The v22 deep executor uses the accepted exact threshold bridge rather than constructing the Fibonacci pair on every primitive. Let $b_t=\#_B(W_t)$, and define:

$$
\lambda=\frac{\log 2}{\log\varphi},
\qquad
\beta=\frac{\log 5}{2\log\varphi}-\frac32.
$$

For $j\le2$, the target cumulative $B$\-count is $1$. For $j\ge3$:

$$
\tau(j,k)=
\begin{cases}
\left\lfloor\lambda j+\beta\right\rfloor,
&k<N(A)-1,\\[2mm]
\left\lfloor\lambda j+\beta\right\rfloor+1,
&k=N(A)-1.
\end{cases}
$$

The compact selector is:

```text
if b_t < tau(j,k):      B
else if k < N(A)-1:     Q
else:                   L
```

The native runner encloses $\lambda$ and $\beta$ with certified rational lower and upper bounds at 96 fractional bits. It accepts a floor or terminal ceiling only when both outward-rounded decisions agree. The billion-transition run produced zero ambiguous decisions.

This compact selector preserves the exact primitive recurrence and aggregate retained geometry. It does not replace the points, charts, transfers, or primary relation with their counts. Exact finite geometry is generated by the local $B/Q/L$ laws in Sections 3 and 4; the cardinality descriptors are exact readouts of that geometry.

## 6. Causal progression and endogenous time

A cause opens a bounded local transaction. The cause identifies the participating state and intended world consequences. It does not choose the primitive.

For one local cell:

```text
admitted cause
-> local custody independently selects B, Q, or L
-> complete lifted object is staged
-> typed local world consequences are staged
-> both publish or neither publishes
```

Failure leaves the complete prior state unchanged. `NO_WORK` leaves the complete prior state unchanged.

For a multi-cell cause:

- every participant supplies its exact current version;
- stale work is rejected before mutation;
- each participant independently selects its own primitive;
- only the participating cells are staged;
- every participant publishes or none publishes;
- nonparticipants are not evaluated or copied.

Global history is a partial order of local commits and explicit cross-cell receipts. A local commit identifier is evidence that a local transition occurred. It is not a universal time coordinate.

Different regions may accumulate different causal depths. A dormant region may remain unchanged while another region commits many transitions. Interaction joins their histories through one explicit causal dependency without synchronizing the entire connectome.

Wall time, render cadence, logging frequency, polling rate, and benchmark loop count do not enter the model. Not as scheduling, not as hidden time.

“Observer,” “traveler,” and “cause” are different categories:

- An external reader, camera, renderer, debugger, or observatory only reads a
  committed state. Reading does not become an event inside that state and
  cannot advance it.
- An observer represented inside the world is an ordinary retained entity. It
  has no special clock or progression privilege.
- A traveler occupying a node is retained occupancy. Mere residence, nearness,
  or eligibility to interact does not cause a transition.
- An actual local interaction involving that entity is a one-cell cause. An
  actual crossing is a two-cell cause joining the exact current source and
  destination. Those cells advance because they participate in that event, not
  because an observer or traveler was nearby.

Proximity can determine which interactions are possible only when the
world-generated geometry derives such a relation. Proximity itself is not an
implicit clock. Activity near a node affects that node only when the retained
dependency of the event includes it. Cortex therefore advances by causal
participation, never by passive presence or viewing.

A traveler may carry a problem token and its relations as retained causal state. Placing that token in the connectome changes the local causal configuration and therefore changes which retained work and boundary causes can exist next. The token is not an external command stream.

Retained unfinished work and committed boundary consequences can become later causes. A host loop does not manufacture replacement causes merely to keep the engine moving.

External input enters through an explicit admitted cause. A world-generated
consequence may cross the device boundary only as an immutable identity-linked
record. A witnessed consequence returns, if relevant, as a new identity-linked
cause. Cortex does not prescribe whether that consequence is later perceived as
light, sound, motion, or another presentation form. External devices never
share mutable causal state with the connectome.

## 7. The connectome and its active frontier

The connectome retains stable identities, versions, topology, residency, occupancy, and local causal state.

Nodes, relations or edges, travelers, and carried tokens are first-class retained entities. Storage location may change without changing identity.

One causal fact has one mutable owner. Adjacency caches, lookup tables,
occupancy summaries, and counts are derived indices or observations. Destroying
and rebuilding one of those indices does not change continuation.

No fingerprint or hash stands in for retained state. The authoritative identity
of a local state is its stable entity identity and generation, its exact commit
version, and its canonical byte-exact contents. Stale-work rejection compares
the identity and version named by the cause. Duplicate-cause rejection uses the
cause identity. Neither requires hashing the complete state.

Entity creation and retirement are typed atomic transactions. Unused capacity is allocation space, not a population of dormant entities. Stable identities and generations reject stale handles after retirement or reuse.

Live work is sparse:

- no transition scans all nodes;
- no transition scans all edges;
- no dense adjacency matrix controls live execution;
- no telemetry query becomes a hidden world scan;
- a frontier expands only through an actual local dependency created by the retained state and cause.

Computation scales with the participating causal frontier and explicit boundary consequences, not with total inactive capacity.

Live connectome storage uses sparse fixed-layout structures. General allocation and capacity scans remain outside the successful hot transition.

categories do not automatically survive with it.

- Exact `B > Q > L` priority and one primitive per admitted transition.
- Transient primitive receipts.

- Wide retained numeric state with explicit capacity demand.
- Wordless save, destroy, restore, and exact continuation.
- Canonical corruption rejection before publication.
- Fixed-layout local world state with stable identities and typed intents.
- Atomic local lifted-state and world-state commit.
- Version-guarded two-cell and bounded regional transactions.
- Sparse active-frontier isolation.
- Immutable identity-linked records at the boundary between committed world
  state and external devices.
- Deterministic external-completion admission followed by a separate explicit
  world cause when the returned consequence matters to the world.
- Sparse zone residency and activation.
- Atomic two-zone traveler handoff.
- Empty-origin snapshot, eviction, restoration, and explicit return.
- Retained multi-zone routes.

The historical CP20 effect experiment named render, audio, and media-stream
request kinds. Those names are not retained as engine-level world dynamics.

thoughts or transitions. If visual, acoustic, radiative, or other transmissible
dynamics occur, the world must generate them through its retained relations.
An external device may later present a committed consequence, but presentation
does not create the consequence and cannot feed a mutation back into the world.

“Zone residency and activation” refers to sparse memory custody and restoration,
not audiovisual or media streaming.

Residency is memory custody, not simulation progression. Claim admission
reserves participants but does not advance them.

## 9. Production implementation shape

The corrected production engine retains the computational boundary, not the
checkpoint-era quantity of scaffolding:

- x86-64 assembly owns inspection and selection by $\Xi_t$, plus the compact
  coupled mutation of $\Xi_t$, $P_t$, both charts, and both transfers;
- the existing fixed-layout native C reducers retain the proven CP20 causal
  world transactions while the full Orthad is integrated;
- a thin Rust boundary may provide FFI, provisioning, persistence, diagnostics,
  and user-facing tools where those functions are actually required, but Rust
  has no primitive or lifted-state compute ownership;
- the CP0-CP20 Rust reference crates, checkpoint CLIs, scenario harnesses, OI
  validators, and differential engines are temporary research and migration
  surfaces, not production dependencies;
- Python, SymPy, Jupyter, and Lean exist only in external research and proof
  packages. They are not part of the engine source tree, build graph, runtime,
  snapshot format, or deployed dependency set.

Rust does not select the primitive and does not finish a partial lifted-state mutation after the assembly call.

The temporary harnesses are intentionally removable after the production
implementation proves equivalent behavior. Their code organization is not a
design constraint. The CP20 retrospective measured 1.79 ns for raw assembly
$B$, 1.23 us for a local Phase/world proof transaction, and 44.21 us for the
largest recorded return scenario. Relative to raw $B$, those complete harness
workloads are about $686\times$ and $24{,}721\times$ respectively. They prove
behavior, but their orchestration, copying, validation, and diagnostic work must
not be mistaken for the production lifted-state cost.

There are two compact billion-tick references:

- the v22 C runner;
- the independent OI-6 Rust compact validator.

They implement the same aggregate recurrence contract in different languages.
In the direct paired OI-6 target run, Rust was faster: 212.30 million ticks/s
versus 186.89 million ticks/s for the freshly compiled C runner. The earlier
accepted v22 GCC run recorded 285.82 million ticks/s for C under its own build
and run conditions. These measurements do not establish a language ranking;
they establish that both compact representations are fast exact external
oracles.

The later eager Rust lifted-state engine is a different workload. It remains
useful outside production for finite exact queries, persistence failures, stale
reads, and negative controls, but its materialized full-state transition is not
the Cortex core.

The integrated local cell replaces CP20's reduced active-axis state with $\Xi_t$ primitive custody inside the complete structurally retained $\widehat{\Xi}_t$. It preserves every CP20 transaction, persistence, sparse residency, eviction/restoration, route, graph, and traveler behavior. $\widehat{\Xi}_t$ is never renamed as custody.
